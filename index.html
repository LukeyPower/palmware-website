Croc Hunter

Croc Hunter is an arcade rail shooter made for the GeekFest 2026 game jam in Darwin, NT. Players ride a boat down a Top End river full of crocodiles, holding off wave after wave until daybreak and a final showdown with Big Croc.

Built in Unity for GeekFest 2026, Darwin NT.
